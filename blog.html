Summer of 2026

July 26th, 2026

Wow! where do I begin?. The year has nearly escaped me already. I can barely remember whats what and when was when? Yet I know I have done so much too. Its a good reminder in moments like this where I can reflect and really appreciate having a busy year, even tho at times it was overwheleming. I do feel behind on alot things but Ive also grown in so many other ways. Its hard for everyone to juggle their adulting resposibilities and the stress of the wolrd affairs. Honestly, dont even get me started on the state of the world. I have only one world and that is STRESSFUL. I am not even qualified to speak on it either but damn. What keeps me going is making music and learning new things. This have always been the two things I could count on most to get me back on my feet. I learned how to build this website after all, a personal online journal decorated how I see fit not accoridng to a Squarespace theme. Now there aint anythig wrong with that route, but I'm also saving money. Saving money and saving time, I think? LOL

It is available on all streaming platforms. I am using Distrokid currently and it has served me well. I also find the Soundcloud to be very useful. It has connected me with a lot of people. I am more of a Youtube guy, always have been. I still drop a vlog here and there, most recently was my EDC Vlog from back in May.
