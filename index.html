BEATOBER 2026

Every October I participate in BEATOBER! Which is a month long a beat a day challenge. It doesn’t matter what style or genre, you just gotta create something everyday. No more than 60 seconds of music and/or video. It is a lot of fun and way tougher then it sounds. For the last two years I managed to hit 31, lets see how far I get this year.

Check out the website BEATOBER for more information.
